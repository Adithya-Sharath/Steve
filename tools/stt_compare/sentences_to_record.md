# Sentences to record (suggestions)

Read each one **naturally, the way you (or the person you are recording) would really say it**. These are prompts, not a script to
perform: if a speaker says "barking" for "parking", that is exactly the data we want, and it goes in `spoken_as_heard` in `truth.csv`.
Feel free to swap any sentence for one you really hear at work. Aim for 20 to 30 files, 5 or 6 of them in a noisy place.

## Places and things (words that pairs of sounds could change)
1. Come to the parking near the gate.
2. Please park the car behind the building.
3. Bring the Pepsi from the fridge.
4. Put the paper on the table.
5. The villa is very big, wait at the reception.
6. Take the lift to the third floor.
7. Gate three is closed today.
8. The metro station is near the mall.

## Times, numbers and money
9. Come at five o'clock.
10. Come at half past four tomorrow.
11. The flat number is fourteen.
12. It costs two hundred and fifty dirhams.
13. Thirty minutes, not more.
14. Meet me on Thursday at nine.

## Instructions with a negative
15. Don't come to the parking now.
16. Do not bring the car inside.
17. Don't stop the work, finish the floor first.

## Local phrases and slang
18. Yalla, come quickly.
19. Khalas, I finished the work.
20. Inshallah I will send the money tomorrow.
21. Mafi mushkil, no problem.
22. Habibi, come here for one minute.
23. Wait one minute, I am coming.
24. Same same, no difference.
25. Kindly revert back to me.
26. Shway shway, don't run.
27. Tamam, I understand.
28. Wallah, I am telling the truth.

## Mixed (a realistic instruction)
29. Yalla habibi, come to the parking, gate three, at five.
30. Khalas, don't come to the building now, wait in the parking.

## Noisy versions
Record 5 or 6 of the sentences above again **outdoors, near traffic or in a car**. Give them their own rows in `truth.csv` (for example
`31.m4a` = sentence 29 in a noisy street) and note `noisy` in the `notes` column.
